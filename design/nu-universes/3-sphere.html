The 3-sphere S³

The surface of a 4D ball: x² + y² + z² + w² = 1 in ℝ⁴. You are seeing it stereographically projected into 3D — the same way a globe is flattened to a map, one dimension up — while it rotates through the hidden w axis.

Colour = position on the base 2-sphere of the Hopf map S³ → S².

How to read it. In Hopf mode every circle is one fibre — a perfect circle in 4D that never touches any other, yet every pair is linked exactly once in 3D. Sets of fibres at one latitude sweep a torus; those tori nest from a central circle out to a straight line. (x,y,z,w) ↦ (x,y,z)/(d − w) is the projection; turn a 4D plane above and watch circles pass “through infinity”.
