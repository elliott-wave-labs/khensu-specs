# nu.ears

In-ear listening and calls for khėnsu.ñü. Shown as nu.ears. Code, files and the host use `ears` (`ears.khensu.nu`, address `nu:ears.khensu.nu`). No camera, on the bud or in the case.

Every loudness and battery figure below is the product spec. Measured figures replace it when a pair is tested. AirPods Pro 2 is the bar, not a part we use.

## What they are

- In-ear, with a case. No camera, no outward microphone used as a camera, no indicator light that can be seen across a room.
- Voice access on the device. A spoken command is recognised in the bud or the phone. It is not sent to a server to be understood.
- Calls follow the khėnsu.ñü rule: at least 5 relays, run by different operators. The relays carry voice and audio. They do not see who is talking.
- Playback is a nu.waves object in each ear. Height, distance and a scheduled path are rendered in the ear. The sound is not folded into a stereo mix and then played.
- The person can stay still while a sound moves. The artist schedules that path, or the listener does. The samples do not change when the path changes.

## The bar

AirPods Pro 2, Apple’s published figures (2022): up to 6 hours of listening with noise control on, and up to 30 hours with the case. Apple does not publish a maximum sound level.

- Louder than AirPods Pro 2 at the same volume step, without raising distortion.
- Longer than 6 hours of nu.waves playback on one charge, spatial rendering on.
- Longer than 30 hours with the case.
- The case charges the buds. It has no camera and no always-on microphone.

## Why the spatial image can be higher

A stereo Bluetooth bounce is one mix, already collapsed. nu.ears receive the objects: each instrument, its place, and the room. The ear renders them. A scheduled move is a few numbers beside the sound, so it does not cost another channel and it does not requantise the samples. That is the lossless part. The listening score of the codec itself is nu.fidelity, and it is separate from this path.
